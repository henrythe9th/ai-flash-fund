Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Flash Fund?

+

AI Flash Fund is the first structured and open platform for investing in AI-enabled entrepreneurs. We provide $100k fast funding for AI-Native founders, especially those without traditional tech backgrounds who are building with low-code tools.

What type of company or project qualifies?

+

Any software or agent company that provides real value to end users qualifies. While we have a focus on AI-native and low-code companies, traditional SaaS and consumer companies are also welcome. The key factor is delivering genuine value to your users.

How do you select companies to fund?

+

Our selection process is based solely on whether your company provides real value to end users and your traction and growth during the 100-day period. There's no song and dance or arbitrary VC decision making - just objective metrics that demonstrate your business potential.

How does the funding process work?

+

Our process is simple: submit your low-code app URL, connect your Stripe account, and if you make an additional $100k ARR (run-rate) in 100 days, we'll wire you a $100k investment. No pitch decks, no fluff, no ghosting.

How is the additional $100k ARR calculated?

+

The additional $100k ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) is calculated based on the growth of your monthly revenue run-rate during the 100-day period. For example, if you start at $0 and reach a $8,333/month run-rate (which equates to $100k ARR), you qualify. Similarly, if you start at $8,333/month ($100k ARR) and grow to $16,666/month ($200k ARR), you also qualify. It's about demonstrating an additional $100k in annualized revenue potential, regardless of your starting point.

What platforms do you support?

+

We currently support Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Vercel/v0, and more low-code platforms. If you're building with a different platform, feel free to submit your application and we'll evaluate it.

Do you take equity in my company?

+

No, we don't take any equity in your business. You keep 100% ownership of your company and remain in full control. Our investment is based on a simple 10% revenue share through your Stripe account, capped at 10x over 10 years (whichever comes first).

Is this a debt or loan product?

+

No, this is not a debt or loan product. We don't require a credit check, personal guarantee, or any form of recourse. Our funding is based on a revenue share model, meaning you only share a percentage of what you earn. You maintain full control of your business with no collateral required and no personal liability.

What happens if I don't reach $100k ARR in 100 days?

+

If you don't reach the $100k ARR milestone in 100 days, you won't receive the investment, but you're welcome to continue building your business. There are no penalties or obligations. We believe in supporting founders who show traction, and our milestone-based approach ensures we invest in promising ventures.

Can I apply as a solo founder?

+

Yes, absolutely. We welcome solo founders and believe that talented individuals can build successful companies on their own, especially with the power of AI and low-code tools.

Can I apply if I'm not based in the US?

+

Yes, we accept applications from founders worldwide. As long as you can connect a Stripe account and demonstrate the required traction, you're eligible for funding regardless of your location.

Can I apply if I've already raised money?

+

Yes, absolutely. Previous fundraising doesn't disqualify you from receiving funding from AI Flash Fund. We evaluate applications based on traction and growth potential, not your fundraising history.
